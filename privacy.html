SZYMON SCHEDULER

Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

Szymon Scheduler ("the app") is a personal tool operated by Szymon Cabaj. Its only user is its owner, who uses it to publish his own content to his own social media accounts. This policy explains what the app accesses and how that information is handled.

What the app accesses

  • Account connections. When the owner connects his Instagram, Facebook Page or TikTok account, the platform gives the app an access token and basic profile details, such as the account name, account ID and profile picture.
  • Content to publish. Videos, photos and captions the owner chooses to post.
  • Post results. The ID, link and status of each post, plus basic performance data the platforms provide for the owner's own posts, such as views and when followers are online.

How it is used

This information is used only to publish and schedule the owner's posts, show their status, and suggest good times to post. It is never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared with third parties, except the platforms themselves when a post is published.

Where it is stored

Access tokens and post records are stored on the owner's own computer. Media files may be copied briefly to a private cloud storage bucket so a platform can fetch them during publishing, and they are deleted after use.

Other people's data

The app does not collect information about other users, followers or visitors. It does not log in to anyone else's account.

Data deletion

To revoke the app's access at any time, remove "Szymon Scheduler" from your connected apps in TikTok (Settings and privacy › Security › Manage app permissions) or Facebook (Settings › Business Integrations). Revoking access stops all further use of the token. Stored tokens and records are deleted by the owner on request, or when the connection is removed.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
